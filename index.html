NSW Student News Community.

A non-profit organisation proudly created and operated by Year 8 and 9 students. We provide verified factual reporting for students across NSW to help you stay informed on global and Australian events without the clutter.

Join WhatsApp Channel Apply for Admin

Built by students,
for students.

NSW Student News Community is a non-profit organisation consisting of over 2000 members. We are proudly led by a dedicated team of 12 volunteer admins all in Year 8 and Year 9 who provide news to students who do not have time or cannot be bothered to check global news daily.

We curate and provide multiple different types of news which includes major global and Australian incidents, sport, music, technology, as well as other information that is important for students.

  • 100% Volunteer Operated
  • Majorly Sourced from 9 News, BBC, ABC & News.com.au
  • Zero Advertisements, Zero Bias
