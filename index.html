A non-profit community providing verified, factual reporting for students across NSW. Stay informed on global and Australian events without the clutter.

NSW Student News Community is a non-profit, small community consisting of over 2000 members with 12 volunteer admins to provide news to students who don't have time or cannot be bothered to check global news daily.

We curate and provide multiple different types of news which includes major global and Australian incidents, sport, music, technology, as well as other information that is important for students.

  • 100% Volunteer Operated
  • Majorly Sourced from 9 News, BBC, ABC, & News.com.au
  • Zero Advertisements, Zero Bias
